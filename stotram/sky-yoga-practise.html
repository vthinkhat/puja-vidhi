Spinal Clensing

Sit in compfortable position, key is back must be straight (asana/shukasana/chair) Hands are cris-cross, fingers are interlocked. Inhaling: image energy going up, from mooladhara to head to middle of eye-brows Exhaling: image energy going down, same way back. Finally stop after exhaling at the mooladhara.

Ashwini Mudra

Contracting the anus fully and releasing it fully. Focus on the anus. It is the center for all the nadis. This is independent from pranayama. 10 ashwini mudra follows by 1 ojas breath 20 ashwini mudra follows by 2 ojas breath

Ojas Breathing

1. Moolabandham 2. Curl the tongue upward again top palatte, to guide the ojus to the head. 3+4. Inhale while imagining the ojus is pull up by spinal cord 5. Release/Uncurl the tongue - Focus on the anus instead of exhalation - exhalation if natural so instead focus on the anus. 6+7. Exhale through the clenched teeth with pressure, imagining ojus is pushed to entire body. 8. Release Moolabandham ()

Morning #1 - Standing Position

Standing, legs in V shape heel touching, 6 inch apart Using toe move heels away. -- reverse V shape hands on waist, all 5 fingers facing down. 10 ashwini mudras Move the hands over the head, arms touching ears. 1 ojas breath Release hands, move to standing position.

Morning #2 - Sitting Position

Sitting postture, Vajrasana or sukasana or chair Keep the knees one thumbs-up distance away. hands on waist, thumbs back, 4 finger in front, all parallel tofloor

Morning #3 - Lying Down Position

Lay down slowly on the mat, start on the left side Cross the feet and bring it close to bottom, right leg over left Palms in namaste position over the navel 10 ashwini mudras Men put the hand below the leg and raise legs Bring the head up 1 ojas breath

Evening #1 - Leaning to wall Position

6 inches or thumb-up distance from the wall Hands on the wall, fingers facing up, elbow not touching the wall Heals up 10 ashwini mudras 1 ojas breath Release hands, bring the heels down, move to standing position.

Evening #2 - Sitting Position

Sitting postture, Vajrasana or sukasana or chair Keep the knees one thumbs-up distance away. hands on waist, thumbs back, 4 finger in front, all parallel tofloor 10 ashwini mudras 1 ojas breath

Night #1 - lying down Position

Lay down slowly on the mat, start on the left side 10 ashwini mudras 1 ojas breath
