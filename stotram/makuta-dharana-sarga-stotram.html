ಮಕುಟ ಧಾರಣ ಸರ್ಗ ಸ್ತೋತ್ರಂ (Makuta Dharana Sarga Stotram)

Hariharan

ब्रह्मणा निर्मितं पूर्वं किरीटं रत्नशोभितम् ।
अभिषिक्तः पुरा येन मनुस्तं दीप्ततेजसम् ॥ ६४॥
ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣಾ ನಿರ್ಮಿತಂ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ಕಿರೀಟಂ ರತ್ನಶೋಭಿತಂ ।
ಅಭಿಷಿಕ್ತಃ ಪುರಾ ಯೇನ ಮನುಸ್ತಂ ದೀಪ್ತತೇಜಸಂ ॥ ೬೪॥
brahmaNaa nirmitaM poorvaM kireeTaM ratnaSobhitam |
abhiShiktaH puraa yena manustaM deeptatejasam || 64||
By Lord Brahma (the creator) (Brahmaṇā), Created / fashioned (nirmitam), In the ancient past / originally (pūrvam), The crown (kirīṭam), Adorned and beautified with precious gems (ratna-śobhitam), Was coronated / consecrated (abhiṣiktaḥ), Long ago (purā), By which (crown) (yena), King Manu (the first monarch of humanity) (manuḥ), That (crown) (tam), Possessing blazing brilliance / radiant splendor (dīpta-tejasam). That crown, which possessed a blazing brilliance and was beautifully adorned with precious gems, was originally created in the ancient past by Lord Brahma himself, and was used long ago to consecrate King Manu.
तस्यान्ववाये राजानः क्रमाद् येनाभिषेचिताः ।
सभायां हेमक्लृप्तायां शोभितायां महाधनैः ॥ ६५॥
ತಸ್ಯಾನ್ವವಾಯೇ ರಾಜಾನಃ ಕ್ರಮಾದ್ ಯೇನಾಭಿಷೇಚಿತಾಃ ।
ಸಭಾಯಾಂ ಹೇಮಕ್ಲೃಪ್ತಾಯಾಂ ಶೋಭಿತಾಯಾಂ ಮಹಾಧನೈಃ ॥ ೬೫॥
tasyaanvavaaye raajaanaH kramaad yenaabhiShecitaaH |
sabhaayaaM hemakLRuptaayaaM SobhitaayaaM mahaadhanaiH || 65||
His (King Manu's) (tasya), In the lineage / dynasty (the Ikshvaku or Solar dynasty) (anvavāye), The kings (raajaanaH), In succession / one after the other (kramaad), By which (crown) (yena), Were consecrated / crowned (abhiṣecitāḥ), In the assembly hall (sabhāyām), Crafted and decorated with pure gold (hema-kl̥ptāyām), Graced / made beautiful (śobhitāyām), By great people / noble citizens and elders (mahājanaiḥ). By that very same crown, the subsequent kings in Manu's dynasty were crowned one after another in succession, inside the grand assembly hall that was crafted of gold and graced by the presence of noble citizens.
रत्नैर्नानाविधैश्चैव चित्रितायां सुशोभनैः ।
नानारत्नमये पीठे कल्पयित्वा यथाविधि ॥ ६६॥
ರತ್ನೈರ್ನಾನಾವಿಧೈಶ್ಚೈವ ಚಿತ್ರಿತಾಯಾಂ ಸುಶೋಭನೈಃ ।
ನಾನಾರತ್ನಮಯೇ ಪೀಠೇ ಕಲ್ಪಯಿತ್ವಾ ಯಥಾವಿಧಿ ॥ ೬೬॥
ratnairnaanaavidhaiScaiva citritaayaaM suSobhanaiH |
naanaaratnamaye peeThe kalpayitvaa yathaavidhi || 66||
With gems (ratnaiḥ), Of various kinds (nānā-vidhaiḥ), And indeed (ca eva), Inlaid / decorated colorfully (citritāyām), Extremely beautiful (su-śobhanaiḥ), Studded with diverse types of precious stones (nānā-ratna-maye), On the throne / seat (pīṭhe), Having seated / placed (Lord Rama) (kalpayitvā), According to the prescribed scriptural rituals (yathā-vidhi). In that incredibly beautiful hall inlaid with various kinds of magnificent gems, Lord Rama was ritually and properly seated upon a grand throne that was entirely studded with diverse precious stones.
किरीटेन ततः पश्चाद् वसिष्ठेन महात्मना ।
ऋत्विग्भिर्भूषणैश्चैव समयोक्ष्यत राघवः ॥ ६७॥
ಕಿರೀಟೇನ ತತಃ ಪಶ್ಚಾದ್ ವಸಿಷ್ಠೇನ ಮಹಾತ್ಮನಾ ।
ಋತ್ವಿಗ್ಭಿರ್ಭೂಷಣೈಶ್ಚೈವ ಸಮಯೋಕ್ಷ್ಯತ ರಾಘವಃ ॥ ೬೭॥
kireeTena tataH paScaad vasiShThena mahaatmanaa |
RutvigbhirbhooShaNaiScaiva samayokShyata raaghavaH || 67||
With that divine crown (kireeTena), Thereafter / immediately after (tataH paScaad), By Sage Vasishtha (vasiShThena), The high-souled / great-minded one (mahaatmanaa), Along with the Vedic priests (RutvigbhirbhooShaNaiScaiva), With royal ornaments (bhooShaNaiScaiva), And also (ca eva), Was adorned / bound / invested with authority (samayokShyata), Raghava (Lord Rama, the descendant of Raghu) (raaghavaH). Thereupon, the high-souled Sage Vasishtha, along with the other sacred Vedic priests, adorned Raghava (Lord Rama) with that divine crown and invested him with the royal ornaments of the king.
