ಶ್ರೀ ವೀರಭದ್ರ ಕುಲದೇವರ ಪಂಚೋಪಚಾರ ಪೂಜಾ ವಿಧಿ (Sri Veerabhadra Kuladeva Panchopachara Puja Vidhi)

Traditional household Panchopachara worship to Lord Sri Veerabhadra, the family's Kuladeva, seeking divine protection, strength, dharma and family welfare.

Preparation Before Starting

Items required:

  • A clean lamp (dīpa) with ghee or sesame oil.
  • Clean water in a small vessel with a spoon (uddharaṇī).
  • Vibhūti (sacred ash).
  • Sandalwood paste (gandha).
  • Fresh flowers or Bilva leaves.
  • Incense (dhūpa).
  • Camphor (karpūra), if available.
  • A simple vegetarian food offering (naivedya), such as fruit, coconut, jaggery or cooked rice.
  • Akṣata (unbroken rice).
  • A small plate for receiving water and offerings.
  • A bell, if available.
  • Kumkum, optional.

Posture: Sit on a clean mat facing East or North, if convenient.

Deity: Place the established image or photograph of Sri Veerabhadra on a clean altar.

Akṣata: Keep a few grains of rice and a flower ready for the saṅkalpa.

Important distinction: This is a Smārta-style domestic Panchopachara worship procedure. It is not a claim to reproduce a particular temple Āgama or hereditary family paddhati. Any established family or ancestral temple custom takes precedence.

Step 1: Ācamana – Self-Purification

Take a little clean water in the right palm. Sip three small quantities, one for each mantra. Then wash your hands.

ॐ केशवाय नमः । ॐ नारायणाय नमः । ॐ माधवाय नमः ॥
ಓಂ ಕೇಶವಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಓಂ ನಾರಾಯಣಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಓಂ ಮಾಧವಾಯ ನಮಃ ॥
Oṃ Keśavāya Namaḥ | Oṃ Nārāyaṇāya Namaḥ | Oṃ Mādhavāya Namaḥ ||

This is a commonly used simplified three-name ācamana form. Different ritual traditions have more elaborate procedures.

Step 2: Gaṇapati Smaraṇa – Remembrance of Gaṇapati

Remember Lord Gaṇapati before beginning the worship, seeking the removal of obstacles.

ॐ गं गणपतये नमः ॥
ಓಂ ಗಂ ಗಣಪತಯೇ ನಮಃ ॥
Oṃ Gaṃ Gaṇapataye Namaḥ ||

This is a direct Gaṇapati nāma-mantra.

Step 3: Dīpa Prajvalana – Lighting the Lamp

Light the lamp using ghee or sesame oil. Mentally offer salutations to the sacred light.

ॐ दीपज्योतिषे नमः ॥
ಓಂ ದೀಪಜ್ಯೋತಿಷೇ ನಮಃ ॥
Oṃ Dīpajyotiṣe Namaḥ ||

The above is a simple devotional salutation, not a quotation from a specific Vedic text. A separate lamp-lighting śloka is optional.

Step 4: Saṅkalpa – Statement of Intention

Hold a flower and a few grains of akṣata in your right hand. State the purpose of the worship and then place them before the deity.

मम कुटुम्बस्य क्षेमस्थैर्यवीर्यविजयायुरारोग्यैश्वर्याभिवृद्ध्यर्थं मम कुलदेवतायाः श्रीवीरभद्रस्वामिनः प्रीत्यर्थं यथाशक्ति पञ्चोपचारपूजां करिष्ये ॥
ಮಮ ಕುಟುಂಬಸ್ಯ ಕ್ಷೇಮಸ್ಥೈರ್ಯವೀರ್ಯವಿಜಯಾಯುರಾರೋಗ್ಯೈಶ್ವರ್ಯಾಭಿವೃದ್ಧ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಮಮ ಕುಲದೇವತಾಯಾಃ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಸ್ವಾಮಿನಃ ಪ್ರೀತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಯಥಾಶಕ್ತಿ ಪಂಚೋಪಚಾರಪೂಜಾಂ ಕರಿಷ್ಯೇ ॥
Mama kuṭumbasya kṣema-sthairya-vīrya-vijaya-āyur-ārogya-aiśvaryābhivṛddhyarthaṃ mama kuladevatāyāḥ Śrī-Vīrabhadra-Svāminaḥ prītyarthaṃ yathāśakti pañcopacārapūjāṃ kariṣye ||

Meaning:

For the welfare, stability, strength, victory, longevity, health and prosperity of my family, and for the pleasure of our Kuladeva, Sri Veerabhadra Svami, I shall perform this five-offering worship to the best of my ability.

Step 5: Dhyāna – Meditation on Sri Veerabhadra

Close your eyes and contemplate Sri Veerabhadra, the divine manifestation of Lord Shiva. Visualise the deity with divine strength, protecting dharma and devotees.

मरकतमणिनीलं किङ्किणीयुक्तपादं प्रकटितमुखमीशं भानुसोमाग्निनेत्रम् । हरितरमणिखेटं त्र्यक्षमालाग्रहस्तं विधुधरमहिभूषं वीरभद्रं नमामि ॥
ಮರಕತಮಣಿನೀಲಂ ಕಿಂಕಿಣೀಯುಕ್ತಪಾದಂ ಪ್ರಕಟಿತಮುಖಮೀಶಂ ಭಾನುಸೋಮಾಗ್ನಿನೇತ್ರಮ್ । ಹರಿತರಮಣಿಖೇಟಂ ತ್ರ್ಯಕ್ಷಮಾಲಾಗ್ರಹಸ್ತಂ ವಿಧುಧರಮಹಿಭೂಷಂ ವೀರಭದ್ರಂ ನಮಾಮಿ ॥
Marakatamaṇinīlaṃ Kiṅkiṇīyuktapādaṃ Prakaṭitamukhamīśaṃ Bhānusomāgninetram | Haritaramaṇikheṭaṃ Tryakṣamālāgrahastaṃ Vidhudharamahibhūṣaṃ Vīrabhadraṃ Namāmi ||

Meaning:

I bow to Veerabhadra, whose complexion is like the blue-green emerald, whose feet are adorned with tinkling ornaments, whose eyes are the Sun, Moon and Fire, who bears a shield and rosary, and who is adorned with the crescent moon and serpent.

Step 6: Āvāhana – Welcoming the Deity

Join your palms and respectfully invite Sri Veerabhadra to receive your worship. Offer a flower or akṣata at the feet of the deity.

ॐ श्रीकुलदेवाय श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । आवाहयामि । स्थापयामि । पूजयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀಕುಲದೇವಾಯ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಆವಾಹಯಾಮಿ । ಸ್ಥಾಪಯಾಮಿ । ಪೂಜಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-kuladevāya Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Āvāhayāmi | Sthāpayāmi | Pūjayāmi ||
Important: This is a respectful invocation for the worship. It does not constitute a new installation or prāṇa-pratiṣṭhā. For an established household deity, the invocation is an invitation to receive the present worship.

Step 7: Preliminary Upacāras – Traditional Hospitality

Before the five principal offerings, water and a seat may be respectfully offered to the deity, following the traditional hospitality method.

These are preliminary offerings. They do not replace or change the five principal pañcopacāras.

1. Āsana – Offering a Seat

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । आसनं समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಆಸನಂ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Āsanaṃ Samarpayāmi ||

2. Pādya – Water for the Feet

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । पाद्यं समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಪಾದ್ಯಂ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Pādyaṃ Samarpayāmi ||

3. Arghya – Ceremonial Water

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । अर्घ्यं समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಅರ್ಘ್ಯಂ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Arghyaṃ Samarpayāmi ||

4. Ācamanīya – Water for Sipping

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । आचमनीयं समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಆಚಮನೀಯಂ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Ācamanīyaṃ Samarpayāmi ||

Offer a few drops of water into a separate vessel. Do not pour water over a photograph or an image that should not be wetted.

Step 8: Pañcopacāra Pūjā – The Five Principal Offerings

The five principal offerings are Gandha, Puṣpa, Dhūpa, Dīpa and Naivedya.

These five offerings form the central pañcopacāra worship. The following actions are performed one after another, with devotion and the mantra of Sri Veerabhadra.

1. Gandha – Sandalwood Paste

Take a little sandalwood paste and offer it to the deity. Vibhūti may also be offered separately as sacred ash.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । गन्धं समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಗಂಧಂ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Gandhaṃ Samarpayāmi ||

Vibhūti is sacred ash and gandha is fragrant paste. They are distinct offerings. Kumkum is optional and should not be considered compulsory.

2. Puṣpa – Flowers

Offer fresh flowers at the feet of Sri Veerabhadra. Bilva leaves may also be offered as an additional Shiva-related offering.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । पुष्पाणि समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಪುಷ್ಪಾಣಿ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Puṣpāṇi Samarpayāmi ||

3. Dhūpa – Incense

Light the incense and gently present it before the deity.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । धूपमाघ्रापयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಧೂಪಮಾಘ್ರಾಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Dhūpam Āghrāpayāmi ||

4. Dīpa – Offering of Light

Take the lit lamp and respectfully show the light before the deity.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । दीपं दर्शयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ದೀಪಂ ದರ್ಶಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Dīpaṃ Darśayāmi ||

5. Naivedya – Food Offering

Place the prepared food or fruit before the deity. A small quantity of clean water may be sprinkled around the offering. Mentally offer the food with devotion.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । नैवेद्यं निवेदयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ನೈವೇದ್ಯಂ ನಿವೇದಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Naivedyaṃ Nivedayāmi ||

Suitable offerings include fruit, coconut, jaggery or vegetarian food prepared cleanly for worship.

Allow a few moments for the deity to receive the offering mentally.

Ācamanīya – Water After Naivedya

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । आचमनीयं समर्पयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಆಚಮನೀಯಂ ಸಮರ್ಪಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Ācamanīyaṃ Samarpayāmi ||

Step 9: Japa – Repetition of the Divine Name

Sit quietly and repeat the name of Sri Veerabhadra with concentration and devotion.

Primary Daily Mantra

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ ||

Repeat 11, 21 or 108 times according to the time available. These are practical household choices, not a compulsory prescription.

Optional: Sri Veerabhadra Gāyatrī

This mantra is textually attested in the Śaiva ritual tradition, including Īśānaśivagurudevapaddhati. It is included as an optional devotional recitation, not as a compulsory daily household requirement.

ॐ वीरभद्राय विद्महे गणेश्वराय धीमहि । तन्नः शान्तः प्रचोदयात् ॥
ಓಂ ವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ವಿದ್ಮಹೇ ಗಣೇಶ್ವರಾಯ ಧೀಮಹಿ । ತನ್ನಃ ಶಾಂತಃ ಪ್ರಚೋದಯಾತ್ ॥
Oṃ Vīrabhadrāya Vidmahe Gaṇeśvarāya Dhīmahi | Tannaḥ Śāntaḥ Prachodayāt ||

The Gāyatrī may be included if consistent with one's family and worship tradition. No particular repetition count is prescribed here.

Step 10: Karpūra Nīrājana – Camphor Ārati

Light camphor on a suitable ārati plate. Present the flame respectfully before the deity. Ring the bell gently if available.

Śiva Stuti – Karpūragauraṃ

कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम् । सदा वसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि ॥
ಕರ್ಪೂರಗೌರಂ ಕರುಣಾವತಾರಂ ಸಂಸಾರಸಾರಂ ಭುಜಗೇಂದ್ರಹಾರಮ್ । ಸದಾ ವಸಂತಂ ಹೃದಯಾರವಿಂದೇ ಭವಂ ಭವಾನೀಸಹಿತಂ ನಮಾಮಿ ॥
Karpūragauraṃ Karuṇāvatāraṃ Saṃsārasāraṃ Bhujagendrahāram | Sadā Vasantaṃ Hṛdayāravinde Bhavaṃ Bhavānīsahitaṃ Namāmi ||

This is a devotional prayer to Lord Shiva. It is suitable as a Shiva remembrance during Veerabhadra worship, rather than being represented as a Veerabhadra-specific mantra.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः । कर्पूरनीराजनं दर्शयामि ॥
ಓಂ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಾಯ ನಮಃ । ಕರ್ಪೂರನೀರಾಜನಂ ದರ್ಶಯಾಮಿ ॥
Oṃ Śrī-Vīrabhadrāya Namaḥ | Karpūra-nīrājanaṃ Darśayāmi ||

Step 11: Pradakṣiṇa – Circumambulation

If space permits, perform three pradakṣiṇas around the altar, keeping the deity to your right. Alternatively, offer mental pradakṣiṇa.

यानि कानि च पापानि जन्मान्तरकृतानि च । तानि तानि प्रणश्यन्ति प्रदक्षिणपदे पदे ॥
ಯಾನಿ ಕಾನಿ ಚ ಪಾಪಾನಿ ಜನ್ಮಾಂತರಕೃತಾನಿ ಚ । ತಾನಿ ತಾನಿ ಪ್ರಣಶ್ಯಂತಿ ಪ್ರದಕ್ಷಿಣಪದೇ ಪದೇ ॥
Yāni Kāni Ca Pāpāni Janmāntarakṛtāni Ca | Tāni Tāni Praṇaśyanti Pradakṣiṇapade Pade ||

Step 12: Namaskāra – Salutation

Join your palms and offer a respectful namaskāra to Sri Veerabhadra. Prostrate if physically comfortable.

श्रीवीरभद्रस्वामिने नमः ॥
ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಸ್ವಾಮಿನೇ ನಮಃ ॥
Śrī-Vīrabhadra-Svāmine Namaḥ ||

Step 13: Kṣamāprārthanā – Asking Forgiveness

Ask forgiveness for any mistakes or shortcomings in the worship, whether in mantra, ritual action or devotion.

मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं सुरेश्वर । यत्पूजितं मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तु मे ॥
ಮಂತ್ರಹೀನಂ ಕ್ರಿಯಾಹೀನಂ ಭಕ್ತಿಹೀನಂ ಸುರೇಶ್ವರ । ಯತ್ಪೂಜಿತಂ ಮಯಾ ದೇವ ಪರಿಪೂರ್ಣಂ ತದಸ್ತು ಮೇ ॥
Mantrahīnaṃ Kriyāhīnaṃ Bhaktihīnaṃ Sureśvara | Yatpūjitaṃ Mayā Deva Paripūrṇaṃ Tadastu Me ||

Concluding Prayer

अनेन यथाशक्ति कृतपञ्चोपचारपूजनेन श्रीवीरभद्रस्वामी प्रीयताम् ॥
ಅನೇನ ಯಥಾಶಕ್ತಿ ಕೃತಪಂಚೋಪಚಾರಪೂಜನೇನ ಶ್ರೀವೀರಭದ್ರಸ್ವಾಮಿ ಪ್ರೀಯತಾಮ್ ॥
Anena Yathāśakti Kṛta-pañcopacāra-pūjanena Śrī-Vīrabhadra-Svāmī Prīyatām ||

Meaning:

May Sri Veerabhadra Svami be pleased with this five-offering worship performed to the best of my ability.

Conclusion – Prasāda

  • Apply the offered vibhūti to your forehead.
  • Receive the naivedya as prasāda.
  • Share the prasāda respectfully with the family.
  • Remove flowers and other organic offerings respectfully.
  • Extinguish the lamp safely when worship is complete.

No separate formal visarjana is required merely to conclude this daily worship of an established household deity.

ॐ श्रीवीरभद्राय नमः ॥

Notes on Tradition and Authenticity

  1. Nature of the procedure: This is a Smārta-style household pañcopacāra pūjā, not a verbatim Gṛhya Sūtra or temple Āgama procedure.
  2. Five principal offerings: Gandha, Puṣpa, Dhūpa, Dīpa and Naivedya form the central pañcopacāra.
  3. Vīrabhadra dhyāna: The meditation verse is identified with Ātmanātha Stuti Mañjarī.
  4. Vīrabhadra Gāyatrī: The mantra is textually attested in Īśānaśivagurudevapaddhati. Its inclusion here is optional, not compulsory.
  5. Family tradition: Any inherited worship procedure or instructions from the ancestral temple at Vasudevanallur take precedence over this general household adaptation.
  6. Separate deity worship: Sri Veerabhadra is worshipped here independently as the family's Kuladeva. This procedure does not combine his worship with Sri Angala Eswari (Ulagamatha), the family's Kula Devi.

Sri Veerabhadra Kuladeva Nitya Pūjā

Perform the worship with cleanliness, sincerity, devotion and consistency. Elaborate ritual actions are not necessary for a simple daily household observance.

श्री वीरभद्र स्वामिने नमः ॥

ಶ್ರೀ ವೀರಭದ್ರ ಸ್ವಾಮಿಗೆ ನಮಃ ॥
